# MLBB Items v1.5

MLBB Items is an unofficial Windows mod for **Teamfight Manager 2 v0.7.4** that brings in equipment from Mobile Legends: Bang Bang. Build with up to six items, upgrade finished equipment to Mythical, and tune item effects for your career.

## Installation

1. Close the game and back up your save and any MLBB Items presets you want to keep.
2. Disable Riot Items and any older copy of MLBB Items. Download `mlbb_items_v1.5.zip` from Releases and extract it into the game’s `mods` folder.
3. Start the game, enable **MLBB Items**, and restart the game.
4. If items are missing in a new career, save, return to the title screen, and reload until the **Mods** column shows **Match**.

## Builds and statistics

- **Builds** lets you pin up to six items per champion. Smart Builds fills open slots. Use **Force Jungle First** and **Force Roam Boots First** to prioritize role gear.
- **Statistics → Build Stats** shows win rates, pick rates, and games by champion, role, and equipped item slot. Search active champions or open **Match History** to see recorded results for removed champions. Filter by patch, tier, or item balance.
- **Build Planner** lets you compare your saved pins with a draft, see which items will take priority, and save alternatives for each champion and role. You can favorite plans and import or export your library.

## Item settings

Open **Options → Competition → Item Patcher Settings** to adjust item stats or open the **Passive Editor**. You can compare Base and Mythical items, change supported passive values, and see a colored preview before you apply them. Passive settings are saved per career. Clock of Destiny, Thunder Belt, and Twilight Armor also have historical variants to try.

## Equipment

- **Forged components** give you alternate routes toward finished equipment. **Item Info** shows an item’s stats, passive, and upgrade paths.
- **Mythical equipment** has a violet icon frame. In **Options → Competition → Item Rules**, allow Mythicals in every slot, in the first slot, or disable them.
- **Jungle gear** starts with Hunter’s Knife and can lead to Star Shard, Raptor Machete, or Beast Killer. Choose a branch in **Builds**, leave it on **Auto**, or select **None**.
- **Roaming blessings** are available on completed Support boots. Favor, Encourage, Dire Hit, and Conceal use the same slot as the boots.
